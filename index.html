HighLevel vs ClickFunnels

A comparison for marketing agency owners who need to choose software, not a sales pitch.

HighLevel and ClickFunnels both help you publish pages and follow up with leads. They are built for different jobs. HighLevel is sold as an agency platform: client accounts, a CRM, funnels, and email in one subscription. ClickFunnels is sold as a funnel builder that also includes email, automations, and a CRM, with limits on contacts, emails, and workspaces.

What each tool is for

HighLevel

HighLevel’s pricing page describes one platform that includes a CRM and pipelines, a website and funnel builder, email and SMS, booking calendars, workflow automation, forms, payments, courses, and online reputation tools. Phone, SMS, email, and some AI features carry usage charges on top of the plan.

Plans are organized around sub-accounts. A sub-account is the separate workspace you give a client. The pricing FAQ says the Agency Starter plan includes 3 sub-accounts, and the Agency Unlimited and Pro plans include unlimited sub-accounts. The same FAQ says every one of those plans includes unlimited contacts and unlimited users.

ClickFunnels

ClickFunnels’ pricing page leads with sales funnels: a page editor, checkout, landing pages, and A/B testing. The same page also lists email, automations, appointments, analytics, courses, a community, a store, CRM pipelines, and an affiliate center.

Plans do not give you unlimited client accounts. Each plan caps workspaces, team members, contacts, and emails per month. The pricing page lists visitors as unlimited, and it lists unlimited funnels with a “limited time” label, so check that line on the live page before you rely on it.

Who it fits

Choose HighLevel if you want one system for client work: a pipeline, a funnel, and email or text follow-up, with a sub-account per client. Starter is the published fit for a few client accounts (3). Unlimited is the published fit when you will keep adding clients and want to rebill phone and email at cost. Pro is the published fit only if you want SaaS mode: HighLevel’s FAQ describes that as selling subscriptions, creating client accounts automatically, and rebilling usage with a markup.

HighLevel’s pricing FAQ also says you can connect your own domains and white-label the desktop app so clients see your branding.

Choose ClickFunnels if the work is funnels: pages, checkout, and tests. An agency can use the workspaces, but the count is fixed by plan (1, 5, 10, or 20). Read the contact and email caps before you treat it as the system of record for every client.

Recommendation

Try HighLevel when you want an all-in-one agency platform — CRM, funnels, and email — rather than funnels alone. Use Starter if you have a handful of client accounts. Look at Unlimited if the business is adding client accounts and you want usage rebilling. Open the Pro trial only if you mean to use SaaS mode.

Use ClickFunnels when the job is the funnel itself and the workspace, contact, and email limits on a given plan cover your clients. Paying for Optimize or Dominate does not turn it into an unlimited client CRM; it raises those caps.

Neither subscription replaces a clear offer, a list, or someone to answer leads. Check the current checkout page before you enter a card. Trial and refund rules differ, and HighLevel usage fees can start during the trial.
